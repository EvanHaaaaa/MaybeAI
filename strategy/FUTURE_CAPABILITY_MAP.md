## 2. Autonomous Companion Maturity Levels (Autonomy L0–L4, §9.5)

| Level | Name | Control & Approval Model | Current Status in MaybeAI | Required Guardrails & Exit Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L0** | **Manual Trigger** | Creator explicitly triggers every speech, song, game move, or OBS scene via `/`, `/deck`, or chat command. | **`EXISTING`** | Origin + Creator token auth (`SEC-GAP-001` migration to `Sec-WebSocket-Protocol` pending). |
| **L1** | **Assisted Suggestion** | Eve proposes actions, commentary, or highlights (`HighlightDetector`, `StoryEngine` suggestions, `ChatCurator`), waiting for Creator click. | **`EXISTING`** | Sanitized input/metadata (`Moderator`), bounded `ContextBudgetManager` prompts. |
| **L2** | **Supervised Closed-Loop** | Eve executes multi-step missions (`AutonomousMissionExecutor`) with `MAX_REPLAN_ATTEMPTS=3`, `approval_timeout_sec=300s`, and `PermissionManager` gates for `HIGH` risk actions. | **`EXISTING`** *(with `INCOMPLETE` `Goal.reflection` & MC `startAutonomyLoop()`)* | Telemetry-verified progress (no `simulated: True` fabrication), `Goal.reflection` feedback (`FUT-001`). |
| **L3** | **Bounded Autonomous Showrunner** | Eve autonomously selects activities, switches OBS scenes (`MEDIUM` risk), triggers karaoke/games, and marks/clips highlights within Creator-configured budgets and cooldowns. | **`PLANNED`** (`FUT-008`, `FUT-010`, `FUT-011`) | `PermissionManager` policy enforcement, OBS/MC auto-reconnect, sanitized VLM perception (`SEC-GAP-003`), per-WS bounded queues (`INFRA-MED-001-RES`). |
| **L4** | **24/7 Self-Regulating Companion** | Unattended multi-day operation with autonomous sleep/wake schedules, hierarchical memory consolidation, self-healing subsystem watchdogs, and multi-agent collab hosting. | **`PROPOSED`** (`FUT-003`, `FUT-012`, `FUT-013`, `FUT-015`) | **Unattended 24/7 `GO`:** Zero `ResourceWarning`s, `RotatingFileHandler` + `/api/health` deep probes, OS-enforced plugin jail (`SEC-GAP-002`), N-agent `RoomFloorArbitrator`. |
