## 1. Research Pipeline Overview (Horizons 4–8)

Horizons 0–3 established MaybeAI's hardened L3.0–L3.5 baseline (`380/381` unit tests passing, `EventBus` 50 msgs in `0.28 ms`, `ProviderCircuitBreaker`, `AudioWorklet` 16 kHz PCM, OS-isolated plugins, YouTube Karaoke). However, the post-implementation audit proved that **Eve still retrieves flat rows and mutates scalar sliders rather than genuinely learning** (`04_cognitive_memory.md`), **local neural audio backends are unverified in `.venv`** (`03_realtime_audio.md`), **asyncio slow callbacks (`0.11s–0.28s`) occur under sync/heavy work** (`09_qa_reliability.md`), and **Collab is limited to 2 peers without subsystem auto-sync** (`05_collab_protocol.md`).

This backlog defines the structured R&D pipeline (§12) for Horizons 4–8. **No item may ship to `core/` defaults without passing its isolated experiment gate.**
